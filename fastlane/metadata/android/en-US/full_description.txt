WallosMobile is an unofficial Kotlin Multiplatform client for Wallos, the open-source, self-hosted subscription tracker. It is not affiliated with the Wallos project and requires your own Wallos server to connect to.

Key Features:

• Onboarding: connect with your Wallos username and password, or enter an API key directly
• Dashboard: see upcoming payments and spending at a glance
• Subscriptions: browse, add, edit, and delete subscriptions
• Categories, payment methods, currencies, and household members: full management screens
• Offline caching: your subscriptions stay available without a connection, with sync when you're back online
• Two-factor authentication (TOTP) support during onboarding

Built with modern Android development practices using Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, and Material Design 3.

You need a running Wallos instance to use this app. Wallos itself is available at https://github.com/ellite/Wallos.
